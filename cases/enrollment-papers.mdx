After an ACA enrollment, the Marketplace sends a **Marketplace Eligibility Notice** from HealthCare.gov. You can upload it to BridgePoint CRM. It reads the notice and suggests updates to the client's record. Nothing changes until you check the suggestions and click **Apply**.

<Note>
  Uploading enrollment papers is available to **Owner**, **Admin**, and **Manager** roles.
</Note>

## What the notice updates

When you apply a reviewed notice, BridgePoint CRM can:

- **Record the Application ID** on the ACA application of each person who applied for coverage.
- **Record the premium tax credit** (dollars per month) that the Marketplace says the household can use, on each applicant's ACA application.
- **Record household income.** The notice's estimated yearly income is turned into a monthly figure. It is saved on the client's financials as **Household income (Marketplace estimate)**.
- **Create a task for each dated next step.** Each "By *date*, submit documents to confirm…" step becomes a task. The task is due on that date and has a reminder two weeks earlier. If two weeks before has already passed, the task has no reminder. Steps with no date don't create a task.
- **File the notice** on the client's Documents tab as a Marketplace Eligibility Notice.

The review only lists what would actually change. If a value already matches, it isn't listed. A task isn't created again if the client already has an open task with the same title and due date.

## Uploading from the client's ACA card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Programs tab">
    Open the client's profile and go to the **Programs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upload enrollment papers">
    On the client's ACA card, click **Upload enrollment papers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the notice">
    Drop in the Marketplace Eligibility Notice. A scan or a phone photo both work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the changes">
    The review card lists each change the notice would make, along with any warnings. Check them before you apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Apply the changes. The notice is filed on the client's **Documents** tab, and any new tasks appear on the **Tasks** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Uploading on Paperwork

You can also upload enrollment papers on the **Paperwork** page. Choose **Enrollment papers** and drop the notice. It waits in **To review** until you apply it.

BridgePoint CRM tries to match the notice to a client in this order:

1. The Application ID, if it's already on a client's ACA application
2. The primary contact's name
3. The name of each person who applied

You confirm the client on the review card. If BridgePoint CRM isn't sure, it lists the possible clients so you can pick the right one.

## Warnings on the review card

The review card flags things to check before you apply:

| Warning | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nobody named on the notice is in this client's household | Make sure you picked the right client. |
| Another client already has this Application ID | Make sure you picked the right client. |
| Someone who applied isn't in this household | Add them to the household, then review again. |
| Someone who applied has no ACA application | Add an ACA application for them on the Programs tab, then review again. |
| The Marketplace says to enroll by a date | Note the enrollment deadline. |
| The file looks like a DCF notice or a MyACCESS page | Discard it and upload it again as the right kind of paper. |

## Who gets the tasks

Each kind of document request goes to the staff member chosen in **Settings → Task Assignments**:

- Medicaid or CHIP coverage status
- Medicare status
- VA coverage
- U.S. citizenship
- Immigration status
- Household income

If nobody is chosen for a kind, its task goes to whoever applies the notice. A dated step that doesn't fit any of these kinds becomes a **Send documents to the Marketplace** task for whoever applies the notice. See [Settings](/settings/overview#task-assignments) to set this up.
