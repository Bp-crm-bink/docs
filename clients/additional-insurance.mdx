Beyond the health-coverage programs tracked as [cases](/cases/overview), BridgePoint CRM lets you record a client's other insurance policies on their profile. Auto, dental, and homeowners coverage lives on the **Programs & Insurance** tab, directly below the client's programs, so staff have a complete picture of what the client already carries.

<Note>
This tab was previously called **Programs**, and insurance used to have its own **Additional Insurance** tab. Old links to the Additional Insurance tab now open Programs & Insurance.
</Note>

## Viewing a client's policies

Open a client profile and select the **Programs & Insurance** tab. Below the program cards, the **Insurance** section lists every auto, dental, and homeowners policy on file, grouped by type. Each type with at least one policy shows a summary bar followed by its policy cards; types with no policies are hidden. If the client has no policies of any type, the section reads "No auto, dental or homeowners insurance on file".

## Adding a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Programs & Insurance tab">
    On the client profile, select **Programs & Insurance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Add Insurance">
    Click **+ Add Insurance**, next to **+ Add Program**. The button only appears if your role can add at least one insurance type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the policy type">
    In the **Add insurance** dialog, pick **Auto**, **Dental**, or **Homeowners**. Only the types your role may add are offered; if there is only one, the dialog opens straight to its form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form and save">
    Enter the policy details and click **Save**. For auto insurance, you can add the policy's vehicles and drivers in the same form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a policy

Use the edit action on any policy card. The edit form opens in a dialog with the same fields as when the policy was added; save or cancel to close it.

**Carrier** is the only required field on every form — everything else is optional so you can capture as much or as little as the client provides.

## Auto insurance

Records the client's vehicle coverage.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier | Required |
| Policy number | Optional |
| Effective date | Optional |
| Cancellation date | Optional |
| Premium | Optional |

An auto policy can also hold associated **drivers** and **vehicles**, so you can record who is covered and what they drive under the policy. They appear nested beneath the policy card.

## Dental insurance

Records standalone dental coverage.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier | Required |
| Policy number | Optional |
| Member ID | Optional |
| Group number | Optional |
| Effective date | Optional |

## Homeowners insurance

Records the client's property coverage.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier | Required |
| Policy number | Optional |
| Property address | Optional |
| Effective date | Optional |
| Cancellation date | Optional |
| Premium | Optional |
| Dwelling coverage | Optional |

<Note>
Additional insurance is a record-keeping surface — these policies are separate from ACA and Medicaid **cases** and do not appear on the Cases board or in the ACA Pipeline. Editing a policy requires the same access as editing a client profile (Case Manager or above).
</Note>
