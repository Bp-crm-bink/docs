The **Document Center** lets you collect signed forms and photos of ID or Medicare cards from a client without them coming into the office. From a client's **Documents** tab, you send a secure link; the client opens it on their own phone, completes the form or takes a photo, and the result is attached back to their record.

Open a client profile, go to the **Documents** tab, and use the **Document Center** to send a request. Each request creates a single-use, expiring link tied to that client.

## Forms a client can e-sign

The Document Center can request any of these signable forms. The client reviews the form, confirms their details, types their name, and signs on-screen:

### How the client completes a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    The client taps the link you sent. The page opens with no login — the link itself identifies them — and shows the form with their on-file contact and case details for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sign">
    The client confirms the information, types their full name, and signs in the signature box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    On submit, the signed form is recorded against the client's case and the link is consumed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Each link is **single-use** and **expires**. If a client opens a link that has already been submitted, expired, or is invalid, they see a message asking them to contact your office for a new one. Every completion is logged for the audit trail.
</Note>

## Requesting a photo

The Document Center can also send a link that asks the client for a photo instead of a signature. Two photo requests are available:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Photo ID" icon="id-card">
    The client photographs their ID. The photo is filed on the **Documents** tab under **Identity & Residency** as `Photo ID — [date]`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Medicare Card Photo" icon="id-card">
    The client photographs the **front** of their Medicare card. The photo is filed on the **Documents** tab under **Program Documents** as `Medicare Card — [date]`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the photo request">
    In the Document Center, choose **Photo ID** or **Medicare Card Photo** and send the link. The dialog confirms the client will be able to take and upload the photo from their phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client takes or chooses a photo">
    The client opens the link and taps **Take or choose photo**. Their phone offers both the camera and the photo library. A preview appears so they can confirm it's the right card, and they can tap **Change photo** to pick a different one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client submits">
    The client taps **Submit** and sees a **Thank you** confirmation. Only image files are accepted (including iPhone photos).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The photo is attached to the client's documents automatically, and the staff member who sent the link gets an email notification. No signature or typed name is collected, since this is an upload rather than a signable form. Each upload is recorded in the audit trail.

<Tip>
Both form links and photo links are the same kind of secure, client-held link used by the [kiosk](/queue/kiosk) and are safe to send by text or email. They never expose the client's record — only the specific form or upload you requested.
</Tip>
