When you upload a scanned paper in **Paperwork** (a DCF notice, a MyACCESS status page, enrollment papers, a Medicare enrollment), it waits under **To review** as a review card. The card shows what was read from the paper. The tools below help you check that against the paper itself before you press **Apply**.

<Note>
Only staff with permission to manage Paperwork can use these tools.
</Note>

## Filter To review by paper type

Tabs above **To review** show each kind of paper with a count of how many are waiting, for example *All · New client file 4 · Enrollment papers 7*. The tabs show whenever the list has something in it. If a kind has nothing waiting, its tab is greyed out. **My uploads** and **Office** have the same tabs.

## View the scanned paper

Press **View paper** on a review card to open the scanned pages right beside what was read from them. Use it to:

- check the statuses, tasks and names that were read
- spot a paper that belongs to someone else before you Apply

Press **Hide paper** to close it. To read the paper full-size, use **Open in a new tab** above it.

<Note>
The scanned paper is kept only until Apply files it. After that, open it from the client's **Documents** tab.
</Note>

## Preview the documents the client gets

A scan often holds more than one paper. The card's **Documents _client_ gets** list shows each document the scan will become on the client's Documents tab. Every row shows:

- the exact title it will be filed under
- its document type
- its pages in the scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a preview">
    Press **Preview** on a row to see only that document's pages. Press **Preview all** to start at the first one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step through them">
    Use **Previous** and **Next** to go through every document, so you never have to go find the physical paper.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    When you Apply, each document is filed on the client's **Documents** tab with the title and pages you previewed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a task the scan missed

Sometimes the paper asks for something the scan didn't read. You can add that task from the card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Press **Add a task the scan missed** on the review card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    - **What the paper asks for**: pick one of the Marketplace document kinds, or **Something else**. For Something else, type the task in the **Task** field.
    - **For**: pick the client or one of their household members.
    - **Due (from the paper)**: enter the due date printed on the paper, if it has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the task">
    Press **Add task**. The task is titled with what the paper asks for, followed by the person's name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The task is set up the same way as one the scan read off the paper:

- It is assigned according to **Settings → Task Assignments**.
- If it has a due date, it gets a reminder 14 days before.

You can add tasks only while the paper is waiting for review, and only after the card has a client picked.

## Find a past upload's paper

Each row in **My uploads** and **Office** has a link to the paper:

- **View paper** opens the scan while it is waiting for review, or if Apply couldn't file it.
- **View on Documents** opens the client's Documents tab once Apply has filed it there.
