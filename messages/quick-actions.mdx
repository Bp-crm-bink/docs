The quick actions dock is a single round button in the bottom-right corner of every page. Click it and it rises into **Messages**, **SMS**, and **Phone**, so you can reach staff chat, client texts, and your Zoom phone without leaving what you're working on.

## Using the dock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dock">
    Click the round **Quick actions** button (the up arrow) in the bottom-right corner. The available actions rise above it as labelled pills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an action">
    Click **Messages**, **SMS**, or **Phone**. The dock folds back down as soon as you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close without choosing">
    Click anywhere else on the page, press **Escape**, or click the button again to fold the dock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The three actions

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Messages" icon="message">
    Opens team chat in a compact panel over the current page. Its badge shows your unread staff messages. Hidden while you are on the Messages page. See [Messages](/messages/overview).
  </Card>
  <Card title="SMS" icon="comment-sms">
    Opens your newest unread client text. Its badge shows how many client texts are waiting. Only shown if your role can send texts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone" icon="phone">
    Shows or hides the Zoom phone panel. Its badge shows your missed calls. While the panel is open, the pill reads **Hide phone**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Working through client texts with SMS

A text from a known client or lead lives in that person's own conversation, so **SMS** takes you straight there:

- Each click opens the **newest unread** client text and marks just that one as read. Click **SMS** again to move on to the next one.
- When no client texts are waiting, **SMS** opens the **SMS** page, which holds texts from numbers that don't match any client or lead.

<Note>
  The **SMS** page is the sidebar entry under Messages that was previously called **Text Inbox**.
</Note>

### Using the phone

Click **Phone** to open the Zoom softphone panel next to the dock. You sign in once with your own Zoom login, and calls go out from your own line. The panel stays put as you move between pages, and hiding it does not end a call that's in progress. Opening the panel from **Phone** also marks your missed-call notifications as read.

## What the badges mean

Even when the dock is folded, the main button keeps you informed:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Red number** | The total waiting for you: unread staff messages, unread client texts, and missed calls. |
| **Pulsing red dot** | A call is ringing or connected in the Zoom phone while the panel is hidden. |

Missed calls and client texts are the same notifications that appear in your notification bell. Clearing them from the dock clears them in the bell too, so the two always agree — and the dock only clears its own kind, leaving the rest of your bell notifications untouched.

<Note>
  Missed-call notifications now open the client's profile. Client profiles no longer have a separate **Calls** tab.
</Note>

## Incoming call alerts

When a **known client or lead** calls your Zoom line, BridgePoint pops up a card in the CRM so you know who it is before you answer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="See who's calling">
    The card shows **Incoming call:** followed by the caller's name, whether they are a **Client** or a **Lead**, and their phone number. The call itself rings in the Zoom app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Click **Open profile** to jump to the client's profile or the lead's record. The card stays up for 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Alerts appear only for calls from numbers that match a client or lead, and only to the staff member whose line is ringing. If a call rings several people at once, each of them gets their own card. Keep a BridgePoint tab open to receive alerts.
</Note>
