**Internal Forms** is your organization's shared library of internal form templates. It gives staff one place to find the forms they use day to day, kept consistent across every office in the organization.

The library is scoped to your organization — everyone in the org sees the same set of forms.

## Who can access it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="View forms" icon="eye">
    All staff roles can open Internal Forms and use the forms in the library.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage forms" icon="pen-to-square">
    Adding, renaming, and removing forms is limited to **Owner**, **Admin**, and **Manager** roles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Managing the library

Staff with management access can curate the library:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a form">
    Use **Add** to create a new internal form entry for the organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename a form">
    Use the rename action on a row to update a form's name so it stays clear and findable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Internal Forms is a staff-facing library — it is separate from the client-facing [Document Center](/clients/document-center), which is how you send forms to clients to e-sign.
</Note>
