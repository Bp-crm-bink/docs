BridgePoint can show **pop-up cards** that slide in at the top right of the screen. Pop-ups cover three things: new messages from coworkers, **@mentions**, and reminders for your upcoming appointments. You choose which ones you get in **Settings → Notifications**.

---

## Message and mention pop-ups

When a coworker sends you a direct message or posts in a channel you're in, a card slides in at the top right. Mentions get a card of their own, even if message pop-ups are off.

- Click the card or **Reply** to open the conversation in Messages.
- Hover over the card and click the **×** in its corner to close it. If you leave it alone, it closes after a few seconds.
- Several new messages in the same conversation update one card, so cards don't pile up.
- No card shows for a conversation you already have open.

---

## Appointment reminders

You get a reminder card for any appointment that is **assigned to you** or that **you booked**, as long as it is still scheduled or checked in. By default, the first card comes 15 minutes before the appointment and another comes when it starts. Each reminder plays its own sound, which is different from the chat sounds.

The card shows the appointment, its start time (Eastern Time), how soon it starts, the client or guest name, how the appointment happens, and how long it lasts.

A reminder card stays on screen until you act on it. It has no close button and you can't swipe it away.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Dismisses this reminder. It won't show again. |
| **Remind me in 5 min** | Hides the card and brings it back in 5 minutes. |
| **Arrow next to Remind me** | Pick another snooze time: **In 1 min** or **In 10 min**. Before the appointment starts, you can also pick **When it starts**. |
| **Open** | Takes you to the appointments page. Clicking the card itself does the same. |

<Note>
  If you leave **Again when it starts** on, a second card comes at start time unless you already pressed **OK**. Reminders stop once the appointment's time slot has ended.
</Note>

## Mac and Windows notifications

When BridgePoint is behind another window, you can also get a Mac or Windows notification for new messages and appointment reminders. Clicking the notification brings BridgePoint to the front and opens the conversation or the appointments page.

<Note>
  To protect client information, these notifications show only who messaged you or that an appointment is coming up and when. They never show a client's name or the text of a message.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notification settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Mac / Windows notifications">
    In the **Pop-ups** section, make sure the **Mac / Windows notifications** switch is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow them on this computer">
    Click **Allow on this computer**, then accept the browser's prompt. You need to do this once on each computer you use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If the setting says notifications are blocked for this site, click the lock icon in the browser's address bar, choose **Notifications → Allow**, and reload the page.
</Tip>

## Pop-up settings

The **Pop-ups** section in **Settings → Notifications** saves each change right away.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Messages** | Pop-ups for direct messages and posts in channels you're in. On by default. |
| **Mentions** | Pop-ups when someone **@mentions** you, even if Messages is off. On by default. |
| **Appointment reminders** | Reminder cards with a sound. On by default. Use the play button to hear the reminder sound. |
| **Remind me** | How early the first reminder comes: 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes, or 1 hour before. Shown only when appointment reminders are on. |
| **Again when it starts** | Sends a second card at start time if you haven't pressed **OK**. Shown only when appointment reminders are on. |
| **Mac / Windows notifications** | Shows a system notification when BridgePoint is behind another window. |

The **Bell reminders** section below it still controls what shows up in your notification bell.
