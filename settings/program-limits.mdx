The **Program Limits** section of Settings holds the yearly Medicare Savings Program (MSP) income and asset limits for your organization. These are the figures QMB, SLMB, and QI-1 candidates are measured against. The limits change every year (income follows the federal poverty level), so they are kept here as editable data rather than built into the app.

<Note>
  **Every staff member can view** Program Limits. Only **Owners** and **Admins** can add or edit them. The permission is called **manageProgramLimits** and appears in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-permissions).
</Note>

---

## What's on file for each year

Each year on file includes:

| Limit | What it covers |
|------|-------------|
| **QMB** (100% FPL) | Monthly income ceiling, single and couple. Pays Part A + B premiums, deductibles and copays. |
| **SLMB** (120% FPL) | Monthly income ceiling, single and couple. Pays the Part B premium. |
| **QI-1** (135% FPL) | Monthly income ceiling, single and couple. Pays the Part B premium. |
| **Assets** | One asset limit, single and couple, shared by all three levels. |
| **Effective date** | When DCF starts using that year's figures. Optional; it stays blank until someone confirms it. |

<Info>
  2026 starting figures (the federal baseline) are already loaded for every organization. Florida uses the federal figures. Check them against DCF and edit them if they need to change.
</Info>

## Viewing the limits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Program Limits">
    Go to **Settings** and select **Program Limits** under **Workspace** in the section list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a year">
    Year chips run across the top. The most recent year opens by default. Click any year to view its figures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the ladder">
    The **Monthly income ceiling** chart shows QMB, SLMB, and QI-1 as stepped bars, so you can see the income band for each level at a glance. Use the **Single** / **Couple** toggle to switch household size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the full table">
    Below the chart, a table lists every level's monthly income limit for single and couple, plus the asset limit for all three levels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel heading shows the year's effective date (or **Effective date not set**) and when the figures were last updated.

## Adding or editing a year

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Edit an existing year">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the year">
        In **Settings → Program Limits**, click the year you want to change.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Edit">
        Click **Edit** in the top-right corner of the year's limits panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update the figures">
        Change the **Single** and **Couple** amounts for QMB, SLMB, QI-1, and Assets as needed. Optionally set the **Effective date**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save [year]**. A confirmation shows when the limits are saved.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Add next year's figures">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click the new-year chip">
        In **Settings → Program Limits**, click the dashed **+ [year]** chip at the end of the year row. It only appears when that year has no figures yet.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update what changed">
        The **Add [year] limits** form starts from the latest year on file, so you only need to type the figures that moved.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Optionally set the **Effective date**, then click **Save [year]**. The new year is added to the year chips and opens automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Every amount must be more than $0. Check the figures against DCF before saving. Each save is recorded in the [audit log](/settings/staff-management#audit-log) along with the previous figures for that year.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If no limits are on file yet, the section shows **No limits on file yet**. Owners and Admins can add the current year to get started.
</Tip>
